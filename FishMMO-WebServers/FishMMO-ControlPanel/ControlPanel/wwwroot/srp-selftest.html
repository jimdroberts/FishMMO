SRP self-test

Checks js/srp.js — the client half of the panel's real login — against known-answer vectors produced by the srp 1.0.7 .NET library with SrpParameters.Create2048<SHA512>() — the exact configuration the LoginServer authenticates with. A failure here means the browser and the game server would disagree, and nobody could sign in.

Serve this over http://localhost or HTTPS. crypto.subtle is unavailable on file://, and every check will fail with a null reference.
