QR self-test

Checks js/qr.js, the encoder behind the authenticator enrolment code. It is written out by hand because the panel has no bundler and an admin surface must render with no network beyond its own origin.

These checks pin the pieces that fail silently: a symbol with the wrong error-correction polynomial, the wrong format-bit order or missing version information still looks exactly like a QR code and scans as nothing. The vectors below are the published format and version strings from the specification, so a regression is caught here rather than by a player who cannot enrol.

The encoder itself is verified end to end against an independent decoder (zbarimg) outside the browser; this page pins the parts that can be checked without one.
